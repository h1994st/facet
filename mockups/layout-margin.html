The Missing Sock

For: long writeups, investigation notes, posts with many asides

The first step is still a sniff of the scene. Apart from the laundry basket, only the gap under the bed is open, and something is rustling in thereThe rustling is right under the nightstand's shadow, so no guessing.. One look turns up a well-known passage. 

$ sniff --deep ./bedroom
basket: socks ×3 · bed: dust ×12 · closet: box ×1

Under the bed there is only dust. Digging through the box next to it turns up the scent of a second sockIt pairs with the one in the basket: matching things are always worth a look., which moves the trail to the closet.

The answer is on top of the closet, in a corner only a cat can reachMargin notes hold the "why" and "by the way", keeping the text on the main line. On narrow screens, tap the number to open one.. The rest follows.
